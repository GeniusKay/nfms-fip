About NFMS

What is NFMS?

The National Forest Monitoring System (NFMS) is a comprehensive framework for collecting, managing and using forest data to support sustainable forest management, climate action and informed decision-making in Kenya.

Reliable Forest Data
Evidence-Based Decision Making
Climate Change Mitigation
Sustainable Forest Management
A Greener Kenya

Key Components of NFMS

The NFMS integrates multiple systems and data sources, including satellite monitoring, forest inventories, UAVs, GIS and climate data, to provide accurate, timely and accessible information.

Better data. Better decisions. Healthier forests.
